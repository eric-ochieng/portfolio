The problem and why it mattered

Meeting information often exists, but it is fragmented across invitations, chats, files, previous discussions, and people. Preparation becomes a search task, while post-meeting catch-up becomes an interpretation task.

Three consequential decisions

1. Design around the meeting moment

Surface a focused brief where preparation already happens instead of asking people to visit a separate destination. [Verify decision and placement]

2. Prioritize decisions and open threads

Optimize for what changes participation: unresolved questions, prior commitments, relevant relationships, and recent context. The alternative was a chronological digest that preserved order but obscured significance. [Add evidence]

3. Keep generated guidance inspectable

Let people understand the basis of a suggestion and recover when context is incomplete. [Add source, feedback, and failure-state details]
