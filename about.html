About

I care about helping people make sense of what surrounds them.

I’m an EX-Microsoft Product Designer in Nairobi. I’m drawn to complex products, the systems behind human relationships, and AI experiences that are genuinely useful because they respect people’s context and judgment.

[Add approved portrait]

Designing from complexity toward clarity.

My work sits across Microsoft 365, Copilot, people experiences, organization understanding, conversation recaps, and meeting preparation. Across those spaces, the recurring challenge is the same: people have more information than they can comfortably turn into understanding.

I work by finding the decisions that matter most, making trade-offs explicit, and shaping experiences that help people move forward with confidence.

My approach

01

Start with context

I look beyond the immediate screen to understand the workflow, relationships, and constraints around it.

02

Use craft to clarify

Hierarchy, language, interaction, and visual detail all carry meaning. I use them deliberately.

03

Stay accountable

I carry decisions through partnership and implementation, then learn from what the product reveals.

Collaboration

Direct, curious, and grounded in the work.

I partner closely with product management, engineering, research, content, and design peers. I make thinking visible early, invite useful disagreement, and distinguish the decisions I own from the outcomes the team creates together.
