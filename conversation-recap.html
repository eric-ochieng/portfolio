The problem and why it mattered

Catch-up experiences can compress information, but compression alone is not understanding. People need a useful path from “what happened?” to “what should I do?” without losing source context or interpersonal nuance.

Three consequential decisions

1. Lead with relevance, preserve traceability

Prioritize the most useful changes while keeping a clear path back to source material. The alternative was a dense, exhaustive recap that was complete but harder to act on. [Verify decision]

2. Separate facts from interpretation

Use structure and language to distinguish observed conversation content from AI synthesis or suggested meaning. [Add approved content patterns]

3. Design the recovery path with the ideal path

Missing permissions, insufficient context, and low-confidence output need clear next steps rather than a generic error. [Add failure-state examples]
