Three consequential decisions

1. Organize around questions, not the org chart

The experience should help people answer practical questions about ownership and relationships instead of asking them to interpret hierarchy first. [Verify decision and rationale]

2. Reveal complexity progressively

Start with the context most likely to help, then let people inspect deeper detail. The alternative, showing everything at once, increased density but reduced scanability. [Add evidence]

3. Be explicit about data boundaries

Represent missing or uncertain information honestly rather than filling gaps with false confidence. [Add approved empty and error states]
