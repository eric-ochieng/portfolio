03 / How I work

Clarity is a series
of deliberate choices.

Connect the problem, the system,
and the person using it.

01

Find the real question.

Start with what a person is trying to understand or do. Give the experience a clear job before giving it more features.

02

Make the system legible.

Create a useful overview, then reveal detail when it matters. Preserve context as people move between levels.

03

Keep people in control.

Make actions, exits, and recovery clear. Treat loading, empty, and failure states as part of the experience.

04

Bring the team along.

Make decisions tangible through prototypes, clear trade-offs, and careful collaboration with product and engineering.
